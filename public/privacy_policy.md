# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services for downloading and sharing video footage (footages).

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 User Account Information
- **Username/Nickname** - chosen by you during registration
- **Password** - encrypted and stored securely
- **Avatar** - image you upload for your profile
- **Email** (if provided) - used for account recovery
- **Telegram ID** - collected during Telegram bot registration

### 2.2 User Activity Data
- **Download history** - which footages you download (used to provide recommendations)
- **Category preferences** - the types of video content you frequently download
- **Activity status** - whether you choose to show or hide your online activity
- **Likes** - which footages you like (shown publicly if you enable visibility)
- **Comments** - text you post under news and collections

### 2.3 What We Do NOT Collect
- **IP addresses** - we do not collect or store your IP address
- **Location data** - we do not track your location
- **Payment information** - we do not process payments on this platform

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses
- To provide and improve our services
- To create personalized recommendations based on your download history
- To allow you to customize your profile (avatar, nickname, password)
- To display your activity (likes, comments) according to your privacy settings
- To manage your account and authentication

### 3.2 Recommendations System
We analyze the types of content you download to suggest similar footages you might like. This analysis is based only on your download history and content categories, not on personal data.

### 3.3 Activity Visibility
You have full control over:
- Whether your online activity is visible to other users
- Whether to show or hide your activity status

## 4. Data Security

- Your password is encrypted and we never store it in plain text
- Your account information is protected by secure servers
- We implement standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access

## 5. Your Rights and Controls

You can at any time:
- **Change your nickname** - update your username
- **Change your avatar** - upload a new profile picture
- **Change your password** - update your account password
- **Toggle activity visibility** - show or hide your online status
- **Delete your account** - permanently remove all your data (contact support)

## 6. Telegram Bot Registration

When you register through our Telegram bot:
- Your Telegram ID is collected for authentication
- We link your Telegram account to your user account on our platform
- Your Telegram username is not stored unless you choose to display it

## 7. User-Generated Content

### 7.1 Comments and Likes
- **Comments and likes** are visible to other users according to your privacy settings
- You are responsible for the content you post
- We reserve the right to remove content that violates our Terms of Use
- Deleting your account does not remove previously posted comments/likes (they may remain attributed to a deleted user)

### 7.2 Content Moderation
- Users can submit content (footages) for moderation review
- Our moderation team reviews submitted content to ensure it complies with our policies
- We check for copyright violations, inappropriate content, and other policy breaches
- **Moderation data** (which footages are submitted, moderation status) is kept for:
  - Improving our platform and content quality
  - Preventing abuse and violations
  - **This data is never shared with third parties**
- You will receive notification of moderation decisions on your submissions

## 8. Data Retention

- Your account data is retained as long as your account is active
- If you delete your account, your personal information will be deleted within 30 days
- Comments and likes may remain on the platform even after account deletion

## 9. Third-Party Services

### 9.1 No Data Sharing with Third Parties
- **We DO NOT share your data with third parties**
- We do not sell your personal information
- We do not share your data with advertisers or marketing companies
- We do not share your data with external analytics services
- We do not share your data with any external services

### 9.2 Internal Analytics for Recommendations
- We analyze your download history **only internally** to improve our recommendation system
- This analysis happens entirely within our platform
- We use your download patterns to suggest similar content you might like
- **This data is never shared with anyone outside our company**
- Your download data is used only for your personal recommendations

### 9.3 Telegram
- **Telegram** is used ONLY for authentication (login)
- We do not share additional data with Telegram beyond what is necessary for login
- Your Telegram ID is used only to identify your account on our platform

## 10. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on our website.

## 12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through:
- Support form on our website
- Telegram bot support

**Your privacy is important to us. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our platform.**
